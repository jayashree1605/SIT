Questions, Answered.

1. What makes SIT Academy different?

SIT Academy focuses on practical learning, structured technical training, and project-based practice to help students build relevant skills and prepare for technology careers.

2. Will I receive a certificate after completing a course?

Yes, SIT Academy provides course completion certificates to students who successfully complete their chosen program and meet the required completion criteria.

3. Can I get guidance before choosing a course?

Yes, you can contact our team to discuss course content, eligibility, schedules, fees, and suitable learning paths before enrolling.

4. Are the courses suitable for beginners?

Yes, beginner-friendly learning options can help students build a strong foundation before progressing to more advanced concepts, depending on the course.

5. Do the courses include practical projects?

Yes, the courses include practical exercises, guided labs, and project-based learning to help students apply the concepts they learn.

6. Does SIT Academy provide internship opportunities?

Yes, SIT Academy provides internship opportunities to help students gain practical experience, apply their technical skills, and work on projects related to their chosen field. Internship availability and eligibility may vary by program.
