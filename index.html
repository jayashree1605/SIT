Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need previous experience?

Most courses welcome motivated beginners. Some advanced paths recommend foundational knowledge; a guidance call can help you choose the right starting point.

How are classes delivered?

Available delivery formats and schedules vary. Contact SIT to ask about the current options for a course or team-training program.

What will I build during a course?

You’ll complete guided exercises and a capstone that brings core skills together. The exact projects vary by course and cohort.

Are internships or job placements guaranteed?

No. Internship opportunities depend on availability, course relevance, eligibility and selection. Career support can help with portfolio reviews, interview practice and planning, but internships and employment are not guaranteed.

Can I talk to someone before enrolling?

Yes. Submit the short form and the admissions team can help compare courses, formats and starting dates.
